The day job

What I actually do

Retail brands sell in a dozen places at once. Somebody has to decide which warehouse ships what, what happens when the stock isn't there, and why an order silently vanished at 2am. That somebody is the order management system, and I build it.

01

Order routing

Deciding which of many locations fulfils an order, and what happens to it when the first choice rejects. Brokering rules, parking queues, auto-cancel logic.

02

Integrations

Shopify order lifecycle and real-time inventory sync, POS order creation, and financial feeds moving between the OMS, client ERPs and downstream systems.

03

Keeping it up

Event-driven pipelines on AWS SQS and EventBridge, with the error handling, retries and monitoring that decide whether a bad night is an incident or a non-event.
